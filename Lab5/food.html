About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu is an annual food festival that showcases the rich culinary heritage of Terengganu. Visitors can enjoy traditional dishes, local beverages and cultural performances throughout the event.

The festival gathers food vendors from across the state, creating an opportunity for tourists and local communities to experience authentic Terengganu cuisine in one location.

Free entry for all UMT students - show your matric card at the entrance.
